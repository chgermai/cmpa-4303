# Reflection

## Decisions

The first decision I made that had the largest impact on the project was not to use a library such as React and also to maintain the site utilizing static pages. I believed they would over-complicate the site design without real benefit. Instead I opted to utilize VSCode with the Claude.ai plugin to give me the flexibility to quickly generate, review, and deploy code. This provided a productive iteration cycle to test functions and visualizations quickly.

A second decision I made was related to the scope of the project content. I originally wanted to provide a detailed overview of each incident but I found my initial drafts contained way too much confidential information. As I stripped away the problem information I found the incident read flat. I made the decision to make a slight pivot to a summarization of the issue and solution which gave me more flexibility in adding humor to each one.

## What worked

There were several features I thought worked better than I expected. The random post and live search worked remarkably well even on older hardware and browsers. I wasn't sure how well it would function with the static pages. In addition, I think the responsiveness changes for mobile came out well, with a style change for narrow screens that was seamless on a page resize.  
I think overall I am most satisfied with mobile view for the site. It may seem like a minor piece but mobile access to a site is a pain point I deal with too often. One of the biggest complaints is a reduction in features on a mobile devices vs desktop and I wanted to avoid that at all costs.

## What I would do differently

My biggest disappointment was more around the content in the site rather than the site itself. I think I would have been more broad on the content scope and pull in other non-incident data and also expand the scope to other people's experiences.

From a technical perspective I should have spent some additional time looking at options to utilize JSON for the data and dynamically generate the pages of a single template page rather than statically creating the content pages. If I want to scale the site in the future, manually writing hundreds of individual pages does not work well.

## What I learned

I learned a significant amount on coding with AI and the importance of being very specific with my ask. I also found planning mode to be a lifesaver as it forced me to really review the plan and code generated. This allowed me some space to ask questions and correct errors without burning tokens before the code was written. I also got a better handle on responsive design. Although I understood it before I still struggled with some of the concepts and working through this site gave me a greater appreciation of the effort needed to not only build and design the site but also to test everything.
