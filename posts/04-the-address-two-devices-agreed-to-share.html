The Address Two Devices Agreed to Share

May 2019 - tags: addressing, data-center

An entire data center went unreachable because some new switches were handed addresses that already belonged to the devices in charge of getting traffic in and out.

What Happened

An entire facility became unreachable across the network. New top of rack switches had been brought online using addresses that were identical to the addresses already used by the facility's main gateway devices, so traffic meant for the gateway got sent to the wrong place entirely and quietly dropped.

The Fix

The conflicting links were shut down and the switches were reassigned different addresses, restoring connectivity in about four minutes flat.

Jargon, Decoded

Gateway devices are the front door of a data center, the one path everything uses to get in or out. Handing that same street address to a completely different building is a fast way to make sure nobody's mail arrives anywhere useful.

Lessons Learned

Fast resolutions like this one are the reward for a genuinely simple root cause, but the same lesson from the earlier switch address story applies here too, check before you assign, every single time.
