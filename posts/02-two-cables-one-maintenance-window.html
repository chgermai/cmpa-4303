Two Cables, One Maintenance Window

May 2017 - tags: maintenance, redundancy

A provider scheduled maintenance on both of a location's supposedly redundant circuits at the same time, which rather defeats the purpose of having two.

What Happened

A facility lost nearly all of its outbound network paths for several hours because most of its traffic routes were normally advertised from a different site, and the connections between the two happened to be the two circuits the provider had chosen to service simultaneously.

The Fix

There was nothing to actively fix, the outage resolved itself once the maintenance window ended and both circuits came back online.

Jargon, Decoded

Redundant circuits are supposed to behave like two separate staircases in a building, if one is blocked you take the other. If a well meaning maintenance crew closes both staircases at once for cleaning, it does not matter how redundant the design was on paper.

Lessons Learned

Redundancy is a promise made by design, but it has to be enforced operationally too, someone has to actually watch for two maintenance windows landing on the same night. A manual backup path was documented afterward specifically in case this exact scenario ever happened again.
