Four Strands of Glass

October 2025 - tags: fiber, physical-layer

Business apps went quiet for nearly two hours because four glass strands between two buildings decided to stop cooperating at once.

What Happened

A cluster of fiber links connecting one office building to another on the same campus all stopped working, cutting off business applications for almost two hours. Four separate links failing in the same window is the kind of coincidence that makes you check the weather report and then check it again.

The Fix

The networking team traced it back to the physical fiber links themselves rather than anything upstream, and restored them one by one until connectivity came back fully.

Jargon, Decoded

Fiber links are the actual glass cables carrying your data as pulses of light between buildings. Redundancy is supposed to mean that if one strand fails, three others pick up the slack, so four failing together is a bit like all four tires going flat in the same parking lot.

Lessons Learned

Physical infrastructure between buildings looks boring right up until it takes down everything running across it. A short, clean recovery is nice, but it is still worth asking why four supposedly independent paths all had a bad day together.
